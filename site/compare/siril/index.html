Astraios vs Siril

Both are free, GPL-3.0 and cover calibration through stacking and processing. Checked against official Siril sources on 1 September 2026: Astraios 0.1.25 (v0.1.25-alpha) and Siril 1.4.4 (released 17 June 2026).

Short version. Siril is the mature, stable, well-documented choice with a large community. Astraios runs its own engine on the GPU, bundles AI denoise and deconvolution, and offers a guided workflow, at alpha maturity. On the same subs with matched settings the two produce the same star peaks and sharpness; see the measured quality comparison. No speed comparison is published yet.

Working together

Astraios reads Siril's FITS output right side up (it honours ROWORDER), can stack subs Siril registered, and can reuse a Seti Astro Gaia catalog folder. Using Siril for pre-processing and Astraios for the guided post-processing, or the other way round, works.

What Astraios does not have

  • Twenty years of stability and a community that has seen every kind of data.
  • Siril's photometry and astrometry depth, its scripting ecosystem and its documentation.
  • Camera raw support.
